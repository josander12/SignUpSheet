The House Help SMS reminder program is operated by Joseph Anderson to help volunteers remember their upcoming shifts.

About the SMS Reminder Program

Volunteers who opt in agree to receive one automated text reminder the day before their scheduled volunteer shift at the mobile phone number they provide. Message frequency varies depending on your volunteer schedule. Message and data rates may apply.

SMS consent is optional and is not required to volunteer. Providing a phone number alone does not enroll you in SMS reminders.

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving SMS reminders or HELP for assistance. You can also contact the volunteer coordinator using the contact information provided on this website.

How to Opt In

  1. Open the volunteer sign-up page and choose a volunteer shift.
  2. Enter your name and a valid mobile phone number in the sign-up form.
  3. Review the SMS consent disclosure and check Text me a reminder the day before my shift. The checkbox is unchecked by default.
  4. Complete your shift details and submit the form using Add volunteer slot.

To volunteer without SMS reminders, leave the consent checkbox unchecked. The phone number field is also optional.

Privacy and Terms

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions for details about how your information is used and the SMS reminder program.
