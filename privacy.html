This website is operated by Joseph Anderson for volunteer coordination and scheduling.

Information We Collect

When you sign up to volunteer, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, contact information, volunteer date and time, and any notes you submit.

If you choose to receive SMS reminders, we may also collect your mobile phone number and your consent to receive text-message reminders related to your volunteer shift.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through this website is used only for purposes related to volunteer coordination, including:

  • Managing volunteer schedules and assignments
  • Contacting volunteers when necessary
  • Sending optional SMS reminders to volunteers who have explicitly opted in
  • Sending administrative notifications to the volunteer coordinator regarding new volunteer signups

SMS and Mobile Information

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS consent is optional and is not required in order to volunteer.

Message frequency varies depending on your volunteer schedule. Message and data rates may apply. You may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving SMS messages or HELP for assistance.

Service Providers

We may use service providers necessary to operate this website and deliver its services, such as website hosting and SMS delivery providers. Information may be processed by those providers only as necessary to provide those services.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information submitted through the website. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your information, please contact the volunteer coordinator using the contact information provided on this website.
