SMS Volunteer Reminders

The SMS messaging program is operated by Joseph Anderson for volunteer scheduling and reminder purposes.

Volunteers may optionally choose to receive SMS reminders related to their upcoming volunteer shifts.

By checking the SMS consent box on the volunteer signup form and providing a mobile phone number, you consent to receive automated text messages related to your volunteer schedule from Joseph Anderson.

Message frequency varies based on your volunteer activities. Message and data rates may apply.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not required to volunteer.

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any SMS message. After opting out, you will no longer receive volunteer reminder messages unless you opt in again.

For help, reply HELP or contact the volunteer coordinator using the contact information provided on this website.

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
