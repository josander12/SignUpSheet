Hello!

My name is Emily Jimenez. I am part of an exciting project with self-help homes to build a home for my family. Our group of 13 families will work together on the homes untilwe are all finished. I could use some help from volunteers to reach my required hours.

Each volunteer visit must be at least 3 hours on site to count toward my required hours. Shorter visits are welcome, but will not count toward my hours.

No experience is required. I am brand new to this myself, and I would love your help!

Where to go & check in

We are working near the intersection of 350 E and 1100 N in Salem, Utah, in the neighborhood just north of Stokes Market.

You can park on the side of the road. Check in with Emily when you arrive.

What we are working on

Right now we are working on framing. Specific tasks will be assigned when we arrive and are ready to work each day.

What to bring

No tools are required. I have two sets of tools that I can share. If you have tools you would like to bring, feel free!

Recommended tools, if you have them

  • Tool belt
  • Framing hammer
  • Chalk line
  • Nail puller
  • Speed square
  • Tape measure
  • Utility knife
  • Safety glasses
  • Hard hat

Bring water and food according to your needs and how long you will be there.

Questions?

Please feel free to call or text Emily at 801-494-9104.

Thank you so much!
